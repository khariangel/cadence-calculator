How it's worked out

Days to empty

Uses per pack, divided by uses per week, times seven. The clock starts when the pack arrives.

Order by

The day the pack runs out, minus shipping time, minus a two-day margin. Ordered by then, the next pack arrives before the last use.

Reorder window

Opens about a quarter of the pack's life before the order-by day (between 3 and 10 days). Longer windows get a reminder in the middle.

Spacing

No two emails land within two days of each other. When a short tempo crowds the schedule, the lower-priority email is the one that drops.

Afterglow, tempo, rest

Afterglow emails follow the purchase and ask for nothing. Tempo emails follow the pack running down. Rest is the one quiet note after it runs out.
